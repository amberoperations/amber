Amber

A private, self-hosted personal assistant.

Amber is a personal assistant built and run by one person for their own use. It runs on a private home server and helps its owner with everyday admin: reading and drafting email, keeping the calendar in order, morning briefings and personal finance overviews.

Amber is not a public service. There are no sign-ups and no other users. Google access is granted only to the assistant's own Google account, by its owner.
